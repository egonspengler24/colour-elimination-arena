How it plays
  • Every leg has a different level. All the balls, shared equally between the surviving colours, are released at once and travel through it.
  • Balls that reach the green collection zone are counted into the bin of their colour, whichever colour they are. Balls don’t collide with each other.
  • A colour’s bin is full when it has collected its whole share. The leg ends when all but one colour is full; the one that didn’t make it is eliminated.
  • The last colour standing wins. If a leg stalls, the balls are given a gentle shake so the game always finishes.
